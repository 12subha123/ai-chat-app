Physics is the study of matter, energy, motion, forces, and interactions.

Gravity is a fundamental interaction between objects with mass. Near Earth's surface, gravitational acceleration is approximately 9.81 meters per second squared.

Newton's laws describe the relationship between force, mass, and motion.

Energy can be transferred or transformed between different forms, such as kinetic energy and potential energy.

The theory of general relativity describes gravity as the curvature of spacetime caused by mass and energy.
